How the Prototype Works

A streamlined, 3-step awareness flow focused on user clarity, privacy, and actionable guidance.

1
Complete Questionnaire

Enter basic health information, menstrual cycle patterns, dermatological symptoms, and lifestyle habits in a structured 4-part form.

2
Calibrated ML & SHAP

The calibrated XGBoost pipeline maps outputs into empirical risk categories and generates a SHAP feature impact chart showing which responses influenced the prediction.

3
Adaptive Lifestyle Plan

Review a customized 2-week or 1-month routine covering low-GI nutrition, movement, restorative sleep, and symptom journaling to bring to your doctor.
